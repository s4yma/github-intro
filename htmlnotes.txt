Structure of a website--> HTML
HTML--> Hyper Text Markup Language
<>/code -->Any function written in "<>" is called "tag".


Structure/skeleton of HTML:-

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html>
<head>
    <meta charset="utf-8">
    <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1">
    <title>Name of tab</title>
</head>

<body>
    <h1>Heading</h1>  -->heading of something ina website.There are different sizes starting from <h1>(largest bolded) 
                         upto <h6>(smallest bolded size).This tag isn't a part of the basic structure of HTML.Multiple
                         heading is applicable.
    <hr> -->To draw a horizontal line.This tag isn't a part of the basic structure of HTML.

    <p>Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipisicing elit, sed do eiusmodtempor incididunt ut labore et dolore
       magna aliqua. <br> Ut enim ad minim veniam,quis nostrud exercitation ullamco laboris nisi ut aliquip ex ea
       commodoconsequat.Duis aute irure dolor in reprehenderit in voluptate velit essecillum dolore eu fugiat nulla
       pariatur.</p>
   
    *<p> </p>* --> paragraph tag. Main contents of a website is written here.This tag isn't a part of the basic
                   structure of HTML. 
    *<br>* -->Break tag. It's a self closing tag which means it has no ending. It skips a line in a paragraph.This tag
              isn't a part of the basic structure of HTML.
               
    <img src="Catnoir.jpg",height="100" width="100" align="left"> -->Image tag."src" means source of the image.Height,
                                                                     width and align are for size of image.This tag 
                                                                     isn't a part of the basic structure of HTML.
    <hr>  
    <a href="Link of different website">Texts on which by clicking we go to that web</a> -->Anchor tag. Used for links.
                                                                                            href means hyper reference.
                                                                                            This tag isn't a part of 
                                                                                           the basic structure of HTML.
